4\. Assessment Methodology



The assessment uses a structured risk-based approach informed by:



&#x20;   ISO/IEC 27001:2022.



&#x20;   NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0.



&#x20;   ISO 31000 risk management concepts.



Likelihood and impact are scored on a five-point scale.



Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact



Risk classification:

Score	Risk Level

1–4	Low

5–9	Medium

10–15	High

16–25	Critical



Residual risk is estimated using:



Residual Risk = Inherent Risk × (1 − Control Effectiveness Factor)



Residual scores are illustrative and must not be treated as validated until supporting evidence is reviewed.

6\. Key Recommendations

6.1 Access Control



&#x20;   Request documentation of identity and access management controls.



&#x20;   Review MFA requirements and administrative access safeguards.



&#x20;   Confirm access review and account termination procedures.



6.2 Data Protection



&#x20;   Review encryption documentation for data at rest and in transit.



&#x20;   Obtain the applicable Data Processing Agreement.



&#x20;   Review data retention, deletion, and recovery arrangements.



6.3 Incident Response



&#x20;   Request the vendor's incident response documentation.



&#x20;   Review customer notification commitments.



&#x20;   Confirm escalation and communication procedures.



6.4 Business Continuity



&#x20;   Request business continuity and disaster recovery documentation.



&#x20;   Review recovery objectives and testing evidence.



&#x20;   Understand service availability commitments.



6.5 Governance and Compliance



&#x20;   Request relevant independent assurance reports or certifications.



&#x20;   Review the subprocessor list and contractual responsibilities.



&#x20;   Establish periodic vendor risk reviews.
