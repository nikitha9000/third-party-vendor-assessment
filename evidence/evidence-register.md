

Vendor Security Evidence Register

1\. Purpose



This register tracks evidence required to support the third-party vendor security assessment of Google Workspace.



This is a simulated GRC portfolio assessment. No confidential vendor evidence has been obtained or independently verified.

2\. Evidence Register

3\. Evidence Status Definitions



&#x20;   Requested: Evidence identified as necessary but not received.



&#x20;   Pending: Evidence status has not yet been confirmed.



&#x20;   Received: Evidence provided but not reviewed.



&#x20;   Under Review: Evidence is being assessed.



&#x20;   Accepted: Evidence reviewed and considered adequate.



&#x20;   Rejected: Evidence is insufficient or does not meet requirements.



&#x20;   Not Applicable: Evidence is outside the assessment scope.



4\. Evidence Review Requirements



Each received evidence item should be reviewed for:



&#x20;   Document owner and issuing organization.



&#x20;   Publication date and validity period.



&#x20;   Scope and applicability to the vendor service.



&#x20;   Completeness and consistency.



&#x20;   Alignment with relevant security requirements.



&#x20;   Identified gaps or exceptions.



&#x20;   Reviewer comments and follow-up actions.



5\. Evidence Limitations



&#x20;   No actual vendor evidence has been received for this simulated assessment.



&#x20;   Evidence statuses are tracking assumptions, not confirmation of vendor responses.



&#x20;   Publicly available vendor documentation must be independently checked before being treated as evidence.



&#x20;   No certification, control effectiveness, or compliance status is claimed without supporting documentation.



6\. Conclusion



This register establishes a structured process for collecting and reviewing third-party security documentation. The assessment remains in progress until relevant evidence is obtained, validated, and mapped to identified risks and controls.
