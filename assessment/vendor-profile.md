2\. Vendor Overview



Google Workspace is a cloud-based productivity and collaboration service offering tools for business communication, document management, file storage, scheduling, and team collaboration.



Organizations may use Google Workspace to manage business communications, store organizational documents, collaborate on files, and conduct online meetings.



This project evaluates Google Workspace as a sample third-party vendor to demonstrate vendor due diligence and security risk assessment practices.

7\. Inherent Risk Assessment



Inherent risk represents the level of risk before considering the effectiveness of implemented controls.



Scoring methodology:



&#x20;   Likelihood: 1–5



&#x20;   Impact: 1–5



&#x20;   Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact
